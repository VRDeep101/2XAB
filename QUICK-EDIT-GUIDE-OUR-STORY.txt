2XAB FOOTWEAR — OUR STORY QUICK EDIT GUIDE

File: style.css
Find: QUICK EDIT PANEL, then the OUR STORY controls.

OUR STORY label controls:
--story-eyebrow-x: positive = right, negative = left
--story-eyebrow-y: positive = down, negative = up
--story-eyebrow-size: font size in px
--story-eyebrow-scale: scales the label (1 = normal)
--story-eyebrow-width: available label width
--story-eyebrow-font: font family (e.g. var(--font-body) or var(--font-head))
--story-eyebrow-weight: font thickness (e.g. 400, 500, 700)
--story-eyebrow-letter-spacing: letter spacing (e.g. .2em or 2px)
--story-eyebrow-color: text color

MADE TO MOVE headline controls:
--story-headline-x: positive = right, negative = left
--story-headline-y: positive = down, negative = up
--story-headline-size: font size in px
--story-headline-scale: scales the headline (1 = normal)
--story-headline-width: maximum headline width
--story-headline-font: font family
--story-headline-weight: font thickness (e.g. 700, 900)
--story-headline-style: italic or normal
--story-headline-line-height: line spacing (e.g. .82)
--story-headline-letter-spacing: character spacing (e.g. -2px)

Example: move MADE TO MOVE 25px right and 10px up:
--story-headline-x: 25px;
--story-headline-y: -10px;

Example: make it larger and non-italic:
--story-headline-size: 100px;
--story-headline-style: normal;

After editing, save style.css and refresh the website. On mobile, the existing responsive rule caps the headline size to fit the screen.
